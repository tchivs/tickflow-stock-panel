# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AthenaQuant serves individual A-share investors and quantitative researchers working in a self-hosted environment. They inspect governed market data, holdings, alerts, deterministic plans, factor and strategy evidence, AI-assisted analysis, and optional advanced research records. Their primary job is to reach an auditable decision without losing provenance, uncertainty, scope, or the distinction between evidence and action.

## Product Purpose

AthenaQuant unifies reliable A-share data, portfolio monitoring, reproducible quantitative research, and bounded AI assistance in one progressively adoptable product. Success means users can move from an investment object to its evidence, assumptions, evaluation, and history with confidence, while optional modules remain independently available and no model output silently becomes authority or execution.

## Brand Personality

Cold-headed, trustworthy, precise. The interface should feel technically capable without performing sophistication: evidence precedes prose, numbers are legible, state changes are attributable, and uncertainty is explicit.

## Anti-references

- Flashy fintech: neon gradients, decorative glow, casino-like market energy, and motion used to dramatize ordinary state.
- Terminal information overload: Bloomberg-like density without hierarchy, progressive disclosure, readable summaries, or task focus.
- Entertainment investing apps: cartoon illustration, gamification, celebratory trading feedback, and over-casual financial copy.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before assertion.** Show provenance, scope, freshness, uncertainty, and unresolved differences before conclusions or actions.
2. **Familiar controls, explicit authority.** Use standard product affordances; make the difference between selection, proposal, confirmation, and execution unmistakable.
3. **Dense where useful, layered where necessary.** Support expert inspection without flattening every artifact into one terminal-like surface.
4. **Immutable history stays visible.** Corrections, retries, evaluations, and lifecycle transitions append attributable records rather than replacing the past.
5. **Optional means independently usable.** Unavailable modules explain their dependency without degrading the completed core workflow or impersonating partial success.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Normal text and labels require at least 4.5:1 contrast; large text and focus indicators require at least 3:1. Every workflow must be keyboard operable with visible focus, semantic landmarks and controls, programmatic labels, and managed dialog focus. Status, market direction, authorization, confidence, and errors use text plus icon/color redundancy. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; never rely on animation, canvas, hover, auto-scroll, flashing, or color alone for material information. Small-screen controls use at least 44px touch targets, long financial text wraps safely, and charts provide equivalent textual/table evidence.
