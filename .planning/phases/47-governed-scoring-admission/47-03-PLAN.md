<open_question_resolutions>
- **No-edit hardening (research §3):** the manifest `policy` group's `thresholds` field is informational provenance only — the live gates read `admission.py` constants, never `manifest["policy"]["thresholds"]` (no consumer in app/). Phase 47 replaces that loose convention with a fail-closed `ADMISSION_POLICY_FINGERPRINT` computed from the live constants at module load, frozen into the manifest, and verified at scoring.
- **Candidate-ledger linkage:** `run_admission` operates on a `revision_id` and verdicts attach to `revision_id` (admission.py:115,483). With the exploratory revision from 47-01 (provenance carries run_id + candidate_id), the revision id is the stable join key. The candidate attempt table is INSERT-only (no_update/no_delete, migrations.py:2162-2165) with `UNIQUE (run_id, attempt_ordinal)`, so the terminal status + gate trail is recorded as an append-only candidate attempt fact linked by `candidate_digest` to the generation row.
- **Failure vs rejection:** a clean gate failure records `status='rejected'`; an evaluation/chain failure (`run_admission` raises `ValueError` on empty panels, admission.py:159-160) is caught and recorded as `status='failed'` with a terminal reason — consistent with AF-REQ-07 (failure is never a zero score).
</open_question_resolutions>
