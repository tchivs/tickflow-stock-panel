<objective>
Establish the versioned grammar/vocabulary authority for Phase 46: compute a stable, deterministic fingerprint over the complete restricted Factor DSL vocabulary and the expression-space structural constraints, populate the reserved manifest fingerprint slots server-side at freeze time, and fail closed when a stored run's vocabulary no longer matches the live DSL. This is the foundational Wave 1 layer that every downstream plan depends on (candidate digests, determinism, and replay all anchor on the frozen fingerprint).

Purpose: replace the Phase 45 placeholder `"a" * 64` / `"b" * 64` fingerprint slots with real server-owned digests, without introducing a second grammar engine, a parser, an evaluator, or a provider (D-01, D-02, D-04, D-08, D-09).
Output: one new module `alpha_factory.py` with the fingerprint/verify surface, a one-line normalization hook in `freeze_input_snapshot`, and focused fingerprint tests.
</objective>
