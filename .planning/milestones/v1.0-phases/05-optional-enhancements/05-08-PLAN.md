## Artifacts this phase produces

- Strict Pydantic DTOs with `extra="forbid"` for mapping preview/confirmation, evidence selection, and server-owned candidate results.
- Root-contained immutable raw and normalized artifacts whose public descriptors omit local paths and whose bytes/schema/scope checksums fail closed.
- Append-only repository methods for new attempts, same-content/correction lineage, normalized fills, diagnostics, frozen evidence manifests, and candidates.
- A bounded UTF-8/GB18030 CSV and XLSX importer that never evaluates formulas/macros or trusts client paths.
- A deterministic shallow-tree distiller that immediately exports and revalidates replayable allowlisted rule JSON, then discards the estimator.
