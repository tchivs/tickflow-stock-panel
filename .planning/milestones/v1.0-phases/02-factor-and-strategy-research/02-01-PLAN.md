<objective>
Create the independently activatable, safe factor-definition foundation: a parsed finite DSL and immutable local factor registry over the existing operational SQLite database.

Purpose: give every later evaluation, hypothesis, and experiment a validated revision identity without permitting arbitrary code or mutating historical evidence.
</objective>

<contracts>
- The DSL accepts only numeric literals; explicit governed/enriched numeric fields; parentheses; unary minus; binary `+ - * /`; and the documented finite functions `abs`, `sign`, `log1p`, `clip`, `rank`, `zscore`, and `rolling_mean`.
- The parser produces typed AST nodes; canonical serialization and structural signatures are deterministic across whitespace and harmless parenthesis changes.
- No `eval`, imports, attribute/index access, arbitrary Polars text/SQL expressions, user-defined functions, filesystem/network access, or dynamic callable/attribute lookup may exist on the compilation path.
- Saving a changed factor inserts a new immutable revision. Existing revisions and evaluations are never updated in place.
</contracts>
