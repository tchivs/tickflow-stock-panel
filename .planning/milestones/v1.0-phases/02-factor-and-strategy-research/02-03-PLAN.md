<objective>
Implement immutable retained experiment snapshots and transparent side-by-side comparison for completed factor and registered-strategy runs.

Purpose: let researchers compare reproducible evidence without an external experiment server, mutable “latest” result, or opaque winner score.
</objective>

<contracts>
- Every evaluation may persist an immutable diagnostic/evidence snapshot, but only a validated completed snapshot explicitly marked retained can enter the comparable catalog. Failed, cancelled, unvalidated, and draft records retain diagnostics but are excluded from comparison queries.
- A retained experiment stores factor revision or registered strategy identity/version, fully resolved configuration, governed-data manifest, prediction/signal references, metrics, managed artifact metadata, status/timestamps, and model version/provenance where applicable.
- Comparison exposes values and differences for all required dimensions and emits explicit comparability warnings for universe, date-window, horizon, or data-manifest differences. It must not manufacture an aggregate winner.
</contracts>

A completed validated result is stored as immutable evidence but remains non-comparable until an explicit retain operation sets its retention timestamp exactly once; drafts and unsuccessful diagnostic records cannot be retained. For strategy experiments, record only existing registered strategy identity/version/source and server-produced backtest snapshot; do not accept client metrics/results or introduce strategy authoring, custom code, or sandboxing. Expose self-contained retrieval/list records rather than mutable references to current factor/strategy definitions.
