<open_question_resolutions>
- **O2 (resume strategy):** replay-based resume. The factory re-derives its frontier by replaying steps `0..N-1` from the frozen seed; no PRNG state is ever serialized. The frontier is a deterministic function of (seed, step), so a checkpoint is a pure recovery optimization, not a correctness requirement (plan 46-04).
- **O3 (seed-pool enumeration order):** frozen as part of the determinism contract. Order: (1) single allowed fields, (2) unary negation of fields, (3) `rank`/`zscore` of single fields, (4) `rolling_mean` variants over a field with legal windows, (5) small binary trees over field/field and field/literal. Exact order is asserted by replay tests.
- **O1 (complexity limits):** `max_depth`/`max_nodes` are read from the frozen grammar group (defaulted by plan 46-01) and bound the generator's subtree depth/node production here; full rejection-as-invalid happens in plan 46-03.
</open_question_resolutions>
